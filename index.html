Frequently Addressed Questions

Clear Terms. Zero Ambiguity.

Everything you need to know about sample requirements, licensing ownership, revision rounds, and turnaround speeds.

In 90% of productions, no physical shipping is required. If you possess CAD, STEP, or packaging label vector files, our artists ingest them directly. Alternatively, you can capture 6 simple smartphone photos of your bottle/packaging against plain lighting, and our photogrammetry engine synthesizes a sub-millimeter 3D digital twin.
Upon final delivery, you own 100% perpetual, unencumbered global commercial rights. You can run the videos freely across paid Meta ads, TikTok Spark, YouTube Pre-Roll, television broadcast, Amazon Storefronts, and offline DOOH billboards without ongoing royalties or talent residuals.
Every commercial package includes 2 rounds of rapid revisions. Because your product environment exists digitally, modifying lighting angles, pacing, camera movement, or hook text does not require rescheduling actors or re-booking soundstages—tweaks are completed in just hours.
All masters are rendered in uncompressed Apple ProRes 422HQ and web-optimized 4K H.265. We natively deliver 9:16 vertical (for TikTok & Reels), 16:9 widescreen (for YouTube & TV), and 1:1 square formats, complete with split audio stems.
